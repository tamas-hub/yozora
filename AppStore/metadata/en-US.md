## Description

Will the stars be out tonight? YOZORA gives you a clear answer at a glance.

YOZORA combines cloud cover, visibility, and moonlight into a stargazing score, then highlights the best observing hour. Choose a city or use your current location to plan tonight without needing specialist astronomy knowledge.

Features:

• Tonight’s stargazing score and best viewing hour
• Hourly outlook from 6 p.m. to 5 a.m.
• Sunset, sunrise, Moon phase, rise, and set
• Viewing times, directions, and altitudes for five bright planets
• Major meteor showers and their next peaks
• Visible ISS passes for the next three days
• A map comparing 12 locations across Japan
• Night, daylight, and aurora themes
• Japanese and English interface

The iOS app adds native location access, the iOS share sheet, haptic feedback, and a locally stored forecast snapshot. Location is requested only after you tap the button, and coordinates sent to the weather service are reduced to approximately 1 km precision. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

Forecasts are estimates. Local elevation, light pollution, terrain, and fast-changing clouds can affect what you actually see.
